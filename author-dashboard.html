Your access settings

Yours to change, tier by tier · Changing a tier is never penalized
Questions about the book — On

9 of 14 reader questions answered; nothing is public until you publish it.

Live reader Q&A — On

Occasional live session — you schedule it, or you never do.

No access — Always available

Switch every tier off and your book stays in the collection, your attestation stays on its page, and nothing about your listing changes.

There is no private messaging at any tier — readers cannot write to you outside these questions, and you cannot write back except by publishing an answer. Contact details are never shared in either direction. That is out of scope by design, for your safety.

Prototype note: nothing here is sent anywhere. The two toggles work so the choice can be seen being made; they change only this page.

Advance readers

One round, before publication · run by KDP, opted into by you
24invited
21finished the book

KDP ran the round: it chose the readers, sent the copies, and never showed you who they were. What your book page shows is the count and nothing else — no reviews, no score, and no way for an advance reader to write to you.

Advance readers cannot review the book through this round — a review in exchange for a free copy is against the Community Guidelines that protect your reviews too. Skipping the round is never penalized; a book without one simply shows no counts.
