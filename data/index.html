How do you find books worth reading?

The complete visualized results behind the numbers quoted in the Reader Discovery case study — published so the presentation's claims can be checked against the full data rather than the excerpts that fit on a slide.

Method, stated plainly. A six-section reader survey fielded July–August 2026; n = 42 responses through August 4 are analyzed here. Recruitment was a convenience sample in two waves — the team's own networks, then a family re-share that skewed 55+ — so age and recruitment channel are confounded throughout, and no result should be read as population-representative.

We report counts, not percentages: at this sample size, percentages imply a precision the data doesn't have. Sub-questions have smaller ns where respondents skipped them; each chart states its own.
