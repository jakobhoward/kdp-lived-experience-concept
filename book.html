Because every ordinary life has an extraordinary story worth remembering.

In 1979, Rosa Delgado boarded a plane in Manila with a nursing contract, a single suitcase, and no way of knowing she would not see her mother again for eleven years. The Road from Manila is the story of the years between — night shifts in a country whose language she dreamed in translation, remittances folded into birthday cards, and the slow work of building a home while homesick for another.

Written first in Tagalog and translated with her niece, this memoir is for anyone who has ever been the one who left — or the one who stayed.
